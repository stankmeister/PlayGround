How to Play

01

Steer the mob

Drag anywhere on the screen, use your mouse, or press A / D. Your squad runs forward automatically.

02

Pick a gate

Every gate splits into two holograms. Line up with the bigger multiplier or the better addition. Red gates will shrink your mob.

03

Dodge & fight

Saws and laser walls thin the herd. Rival mobs must be crushed head-on. Every few hundred metres a titan boss blocks the track — dodge its telegraphed slam rings and burn it down.

04

Upgrade forever

Coins are permanent. Between runs, spend them on squad size, battle strength, magnets, power-up duration and bounty.
